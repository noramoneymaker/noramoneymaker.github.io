Subtitles-only at $0.40 per minute is $16.80 for that same 42-minute episode: 16× under Rev's human caption rate of $272.58, and 2× the $8.40 you would pay Happy Scribe for machine credits. The difference is a file that arrives already measured against the 42-character / 7-second / 2-frame rules, produced on one machine, never uploaded to a vendor. Against what captioners are paid for the labour — GoTranscript advertises “Earn up to $1.75 per minute” (re-read 2026-11-19) — $0.40 is not a lowball on the work, it is the work done on hardware that already exists.

Worked examples: 20 minutes $12.00 · 42 minutes $25.20 · 60 minutes $36.00 · 3 hours $108.00. Subtitles-only at $0.40/min: 20 minutes $12.00 · 42 minutes $16.80 · 60 minutes $24.00 · 3 hours $72.00.
