Terms

Short, because there are only a few things that can go wrong.

What you are buying

One delivery of the files listed on the store page for one audio or video file. The price is fixed before you pay. There is no subscription, no recurring charge and no account.

Turnaround

Inside 24 hours from the moment your payment is confirmed, for every SKU on this site. If it will be longer, you are told before you pay.

Refunds

  • Nothing delivered within 24 hours — full refund, no argument, no need to ask twice.
  • The file is materially wrong for the audio you sent, meaning large stretches of text are missing or invented rather than misheard — your choice of a full refund or a redo at no charge.
  • Wrong file sent (you uploaded the wrong episode) — a full refund if no work has started, otherwise it is treated as a new order for the correct file.

Refunds are paid back in USDC to the address the payment came from, within 7 days of the request. Because USDC transfers are irreversible, the refund is a new transfer, not a reversal. Ask by email within 14 days of delivery.

What is not a refund: ordinary misheard words in noisy audio. That is disclosed up front, it is what the QA note is for, and the measured accuracy section on the store page says so plainly.

Your audio

The file you send is processed on one machine and is not uploaded to any third-party service. The uploaded file and the working copy are deleted when the deliverables are sent. The deliverables are kept for 90 days in case you need them re-sent, then deleted. Nothing you send is used to train anything, published, or shown to anyone else.

What you keep

The transcript and subtitle files are yours to use however you like, including commercially, on the episode you sent. No attribution required, no licence fee, no per-download charge.

Payment

USDC on Base or USDC on Solana only, to the addresses on the store page. Send on the named network; a transfer on another chain or in another token cannot be recovered. Network fees are yours and are under a cent. Prices are in US dollars and do not move with the token's price.
